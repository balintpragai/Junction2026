# Reach Loop

Reach Loop shows, region by region, where Finnish cancer care falls short of national targets, and says so in plain language for the people who decide where effort goes.

## Geography

**Region**:
One of the 23 areas THL reports on: the 21 wellbeing services counties, the City of Helsinki, and Åland.
_Avoid_: county (as the umbrella term), account, area

**Wellbeing services county**:
The legal body (_hyvinvointialue_) that has organised health services since the 2023 reform; 21 of the 23 Regions are one. Use only when the legal body itself matters.
_Avoid_: hyvinvointialue, HVA, wellbeing county

**Hospital district**:
The pre-2023 geography (_sairaanhoitopiiri_), which survives only in legacy data series. Never a current Region.
_Avoid_: sairaanhoitopiiri, SHP

## Measurement

**Gap**:
How far one Region's value on one indicator, in one year, sits from its target, or from the other Regions when the indicator has no target.
_Avoid_: bottleneck, shortfall, deficit. "Bottleneck" is pitch language only; the product measures Gaps.

**Target**:
A value the National Cancer Strategy commits every Region to reach on an indicator, such as at least 80% screening participation. Most indicators have none.
_Avoid_: goal, benchmark, norm

**Severity**:
How urgently a Gap calls for action: _ok_, _watch_ or _act_. Severity is set by fixed, written-down thresholds, never by judgement case by case.
_Avoid_: status, priority, risk level

## Communication

**Narrative**:
The plain-language sentence, two or three at most, that describes one Gap and that a decision-maker reads first. Every figure in it comes from the Gap itself.
_Avoid_: summary, insight, AI text

**Template sentence**:
A Narrative made by filling a Gap's numbers into fixed wording, so it is exact by construction. It is the only kind of Narrative shown in Finnish.
_Avoid_: fallback, default text
