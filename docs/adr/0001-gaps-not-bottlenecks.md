# Reach Loop measures Gaps, not bottlenecks

We framed the challenge as "finding bottlenecks in the Finnish cancer care chain", but Reach Loop measures **Gaps**: how far one Region's indicator sits from its target, or from the other Regions, in one year. It does not rank pathway stages against each other to name a single bottleneck. That needs cancer-specific delay data for every stage of the care chain. We only have it for diagnostic workup, read by eye from Cancer Registry charts, which isn't solid enough to sit next to auto-checked numbers. Gap-finding works on the published indicators we can load and verify, and it gets to a working demo by Sunday. "Bottleneck" stays as pitch language only.

## Considered Options

- **Bottleneck view on top of Gaps:** for each Region and cancer type, line up the stages and flag the worst one. Rejected for now because pathway stages are out of scope and the diagnostic-delay figures stay out of the database.
- **Rebuild around bottlenecks:** rejected because it throws away the validated Reach Loop design with one day left.
